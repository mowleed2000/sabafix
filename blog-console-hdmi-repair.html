Same-Day HDMI Socket Replacement: Quicker Turnaround When It Matters

The HDMI port is one of the most physically fragile components on modern gaming consoles like the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X. Tripping over a cable or repeatedly plugging in cords can bend inner pins, break solder pads, or damage the Panasonic MN864739 HDMI encoder IC.

Most repair shops ship consoles out or make customers wait 24 to 48 hours. At Sabafix in Thornton Heath, our technicians carry genuine OEM HDMI sockets in stock and perform micro-soldering repairs directly in our store with instant same-day completion.

Common Console HDMI Symptoms

  • Console turns on (white light or blue light) but displays "No Signal" on TV.
  • Wobbly cable or visible bent / pushed-back gold pins inside the socket.
  • Display flickers or cuts out when the console moves or vibrates.
  • Resolution is locked to 480p or fails to output 4K HDR.
